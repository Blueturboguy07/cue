Save meeting transcripts optional — stores transcripts locally on your device
When enabled, transcripts are saved to a local file on your device only. Nothing is sent to any server. Useful for generating follow-ups after a call. Inform meeting participants if required by your local laws.

When enabled, cue will periodically send snippets of your meeting transcript to the AI in the background to detect pushback and suggest responses. Warning: This will continuously ping your LLM API during a meeting and may incur higher API costs.
